La Maison

Luxury is not louder. It is left unsaid — and worn instead.

The Climate was founded on a simple belief: that a fragrance should move with you, not for you. Every composition is built the way a French atelier builds a garment — by hand, by instinct, and by restraint.

We work across moods rather than seasons. Some days call for sharpness, others for stillness. Our bottles are designed to hold both — sculpted glass, a single black cap, nothing that needs explaining.

Savoir-Faire

Made in India, Inspired by French Artistry

Our perfumers train in the French tradition — composing in layers, listening for balance between top, heart, and base. But every bottle is blended, poured, and finished by hand in India, where the house was born.

We chose precision over abundance. Each fragrance carries a short list of ingredients, selected for clarity rather than complexity — because a scent, like a silhouette, is strongest when nothing is added just to be seen.

Shipping Policy

Every order is packed by hand in protective, cushioned casing designed for fragile glass — each bottle is individually wrapped and braced to prevent movement in transit, in line with standard commercial carrier requirements for glass and liquid goods.

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days and shipped via tracked, insured courier. Domestic delivery typically takes 3–6 business days depending on location; a tracking link is emailed once your order leaves the atelier. Shipping costs and any applicable duties are calculated at checkout. Because fragrance is classified as a restricted/hazardous good by some carriers and customs authorities, international shipping availability and transit times may vary by destination.

In the rare event a bottle arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the packaging and product, and we will arrange a replacement or refund.

Return & Refund Policy

As fragrance is a manufactured cosmetic product, unopened and unused bottles in their original packaging may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, less any original shipping charges. For hygiene and safety reasons, we are unable to accept returns on opened, used, or tester-seal-broken bottles unless the product arrived defective or damaged in transit.

To start a return, contact us with your order number; once approved, we'll provide return instructions and a shipping label where applicable. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of the returned item passing inspection.

Discovery/sample sets are final sale and not eligible for return, given their single-use format. Damaged or defective items are always covered regardless of the above — just reach out and we'll make it right.
